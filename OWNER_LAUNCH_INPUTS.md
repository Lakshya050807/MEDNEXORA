# MEDNEXORA Owner Launch Inputs

This checklist records the real business and service information the owner must confirm before the About, Contact, FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, and Refund and Cancellation pages can be finalized. The examples below show the kind of answer needed; they are prompts, not facts or proposed commitments for MEDNEXORA.

This is an operational content checklist, not legal advice. Have the final policies reviewed by an appropriately qualified professional for the business and the places where it operates.

## Owner information checklist

| Information to provide | Why it is needed | Example of the answer expected (illustrative only) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Legal identity of the business**: full legal name of the person or entity operating MEDNEXORA; business/brand relationship; applicable entity type and registration details, if any. | Identifies who operates the service in About, Contact, Terms, Privacy, and purchase-related communications. | **Business address, if applicable**: address intended for public business or legal correspondence; confirm whether it is a registered office, service address, or another address. | Contact and policy pages may need a reliable correspondence address. The owner must decide which address is appropriate to disclose. | “Public correspondence address: `[owner-confirmed address]`; address type: `[type]`.” If no address should be published, state the approved alternative and get appropriate advice. |
| **Customer support email and contact method**: monitored email address and any additional support channel; who monitors it and confirmed operating hours or response expectations, if these will be stated. | Students need a working route for account, access, order, download, privacy, and refund questions. | **Effective dates and policy approval**: named business approver/role; date each policy is approved and takes effect; version/update process; how material changes will be communicated. | Draft policies should not appear final until reviewed and approved. | **FAQ operational answers**: actual registration requirements, resource availability, preview rules, access/download steps, supported devices/browsers, support route, order status meanings, test behavior, and any common troubleshooting instructions. | The FAQ must reflect current features and avoid promising a capability that is not available. | For each FAQ, provide a question and a concise answer backed by the current website behavior, such as “To download an eligible resource, open `[confirmed page/flow]`.” |

## Page-by-page completion map

- **About Us:** legal operator/brand relationship, accurate service and audience description, verified claims and affiliations, and any approved educational-content disclaimer.
- **Contact Us:** monitored support channel, request types, confirmed hours or response expectations if stated, and any public business/correspondence address.
- **FAQ:** verified answers about registration, previews, access, downloads, payments, order states, refunds, cancellations, exam information, and support.
- **Privacy Policy:** actual data collected, purposes, storage, vendors/recipients, retention, student requests/account deletion, contact, approval and effective date.
- **Terms and Conditions:** operator identity, account and service terms, digital purchase/access rules, payment/order terms, cancellation/refund cross-reference, copyright and permitted use, support contact, approval and effective date.
- **Refund and Cancellation Policy:** eligibility, exclusions, request steps, review and provider-processing timelines, cancellation rules, access effects, support contact, approval and effective date.

## Minimum owner inputs before public launch

### Before accepting public registrations

- Confirm the legal operator identity and the public-facing brand relationship.
- Approve an accurate Privacy Policy based on the actual data collected, purposes, processors, storage, retention, and account deletion/request process.
- Approve Terms that accurately cover accounts and use of the service.
- Provide a monitored student-support contact method for account and privacy requests.
- Confirm that all public claims about the service, exam information, affiliations, and outcomes are accurate and supportable.

### Before accepting paid purchases

- Complete the above registration items.
- Approve clear digital-product purchase, access, download/preview, and copyright terms.
- Decide and publish refund eligibility, exclusions, request procedure, cancellation rules, and accurate handling/processing timelines.
- Confirm the actual payment/order data handling and support path for payment and access problems.
- Confirm that MEDNEXORA has the rights or licenses needed to sell and distribute every offered paid resource.

Do not publish these pages as finalized until the owner has supplied and approved the applicable information. This checklist itself does not create a policy or make any commitment on the owner's behalf.
